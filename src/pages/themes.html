Themes

Theme galleries are not here yet. This page is where they will live.

A goome theme is a json file: colours, radius, spacing, fonts and an optional skin with icons. The builder has a theme tab that exports them, and Themes the simple way shows how to load one from your own code.

What is planned

  • Browse themes with a live preview that runs real goome in your tab
  • Download a theme as a file, or copy one line to load it
  • Upload your own
